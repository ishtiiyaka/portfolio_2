SolidWorks & Fusion 360

Parametric Assemblies & DFM
01

My mechanical design workflow bypasses basic aesthetic modeling in favor of strict, data-driven engineering. I utilize Top-Down Assembly techniques to ensure complex robotic linkages inherit dimensions dynamically, eliminating clearance errors upon fabrication.

Before a single piece is manufactured, designs undergo rigorous Finite Element Analysis (FEA). I simulate static loads and kinematic stress states to identify structural bottlenecks and yielding points.

  • Generative Design: Utilizing AI-driven topological optimization to reduce end-effector weight while maintaining structural rigidity ratios.
  • Kinematic Linkages: Inverse kinematics visualization and joint collision detection directly within the assembly environment.
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM): Modeling specific to the limitations of FDM/SLA printing or CNC machining—accounting for shrinkage, bridging limits, and toolpath clearances.

Additive Fabrication

High-Speed CoreXY & Polymer Science
02

Operating high-speed CoreXY systems (Bambu Lab / QIDI Tech) requires an intimate understanding of polymer thermodynamics. Printing standard PLA is trivial; I specialize in engineering-grade materials like Carbon Fiber-filled Nylon (PA-CF), ABS/ASA, and PETG for high-torque robotic actuators.

This involves deep dive slicer optimization (OrcaSlicer/BambuStudio). I tune volumetric flow rates (mm³/s), calibrate Linear Advance (K-factor) to eliminate corner bulging at 300+ mm/s, and adjust Input Shaping resonance compensations.

By manipulating layer adhesion thermal thresholds and infill geometries (Gyroid vs Cubic for multi-directional stress), I fabricate components that rival injection-molded parts in tensile strength.

TM4C123G & Embedded

Bare-Metal ARM Cortex-M4F Architecture
03

My embedded systems philosophy rejects the bloated abstractions of Arduino. I write Bare-Metal C directly manipulating system registers on the Texas Instruments TM4C123GH6PM microcontroller (32-bit ARM Cortex-M4F).

This allows for microsecond-level determinism. I leverage the Nested Vector Interrupt Controller (NVIC) for precise, preemptive multitasking without needing an RTOS. I develop custom drivers for internal peripherals: Timers, ADC, UART, and I2C.

Actuation Control: I implement advanced motor control theory, including Field Oriented Control (FOC) and Space Vector PWM (SVPWM) to drive BLDC (Brushless DC) motors. By calculating Clarke and Park transformations in real-time using the MCU's hardware Floating-Point Unit (FPU), I achieve silent, ultra-high-torque actuation.

UART & Protocol Sniffing

Hardware Reverse-Engineering
04

Interfacing with undocumented, proprietary mainboards (such as those in Chinese robotic vacuums) requires intensive reverse engineering. Using tools like Saleae Logic Analyzers and oscilloscopes, I physically tap into unlabeled test points to deduce hardware buses (UART, I2C, SPI).

The process is meticulous: analyzing bit-widths to deduce baud rates (e.g., non-standard 115200 or 256000 bps), capturing data streams, and performing hex analysis to crack the packet framing.

  • Packet Decoding: Identifying preamble bytes, payload structures, and calculating proprietary CRC/Checksum algorithms to validate data.
  • Payload Injection: Building custom bridge microcontrollers to act as Man-in-the-Middle (MITM), actively modifying command bytes in real-time to hijack system behavior.

MPU6050 & Sensor Fusion

Spatial Orientation & Current Telemetry
05

Raw physical data is inherently noisy and subject to drift. I design systems that rely on advanced Sensor Fusion mathematics to create stable environmental truths.

For spatial orientation (MPU6050 6-DoF IMU), I bypass simple Euler angle calculations (which suffer from Gimbal Lock) and implement Quaternion mathematics. By combining accelerometer and gyroscope data through a Complementary or Kalman Filter, I extract rock-solid Pitch/Roll/Yaw metrics crucial for self-balancing systems.

Power Telemetry: Using Hall-effect based current sensors (ACS712), I monitor real-time phase currents of motors. By tuning hardware Op-Amps and MCU ADC resolutions, I create microsecond-fast software safety limits that detect motor stalls and mechanical blockages before components physically fail.

PCB & Schematic Design

Moving Beyond Prototypes
06

Breadboards are for ideation; custom PCBs are for deployment. I complete the hardware lifecycle by translating tangled prototype wires into highly reliable, professionally routed printed circuit boards.

Using KiCad / EasyEDA, I capture schematics, select surface mount components (SMD), and design multi-layer boards. Careful attention is paid to power delivery networks (PDN) ensuring separate copper pours for logic (3.3V) and noisy motor power (12V-24V) to prevent ground loops and EMI interference.

This holistic approach means my mechatronic systems aren't just fragile lab experiments; they are self-contained, robust units ready for real-world environmental stress.
