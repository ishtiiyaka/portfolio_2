I build systems that move and interfaces that feel alive. Emerging from the high-altitude terrains of Gilgit-Baltistan, I now pursue Mechatronics and Control Engineering at UET Lahore, where I sit at the precise intersection of machines and software.

I don't just write scripts — I reverse-engineer proprietary hardware protocols, design precision 3D-printed components, and craft production-grade web experiences that refuse to look like everything else. Every project is a complete, end-to-end ecosystem.

Fluent in English, Urdu, and Burushaski. Currently acquiring Mandarin (HSK). Running Kubuntu with a custom Zsh + Powerlevel10k environment, because the tools matter.
